Nanu Kitchen Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Nanu Kitchen is a single-player cooking game for Android. This page explains what data the game uses.

Your game progress

Your game progress is saved only on your device. We do not have accounts and we do not upload your save. Uninstalling the game removes its data.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address) and information about how ads are shown, to show and measure ads. Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first, and can change their choice at any time from Settings > PRIVACY in the game. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details.

What we do not do

We do not collect your name, email address or contacts, and we do not sell personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
